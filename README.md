📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, and job role trends. The dashboard enables HR managers and business leaders to monitor key workforce metrics, identify the major reasons behind employee attrition, and support data-driven HR decision-making.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of employee statistics through interactive visualizations and KPIs, helping organizations improve employee retention and workforce planning.

🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges for organizations. High turnover increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and affects overall business performance.

This dashboard helps answer important business questions such as:

Which employees are leaving the company most frequently?
Which salary group has the highest attrition?
Which age group experiences the highest turnover?
Which education field contributes the most to attrition?
Which job roles are most affected?
How does attrition vary by gender and department?

💡 Business Insights

The dashboard reveals several important workforce trends:

Employees earning up to 5K salary experience the highest attrition.
The 26–35 years age group has the highest employee turnover.
Employees with a Life Sciences educational background contribute the largest share of attrition.
Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition among all job roles.
Male employees show higher attrition than female employees.
Most employees leave within the early years of joining the company.
HR managers can use these insights to improve employee retention strategies, compensation planning, and workforce management.
